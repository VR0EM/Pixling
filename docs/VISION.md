# Vision

Pixling is a little pixel buddy that really lives on your desktop. It joins in with what you do and helps through its behaviour instead of notifications. Everything about it is yours: its looks, character, features and behaviour.

## Four pillars

**1. It's alive: your desktop is its world.**
It walks along the bottom of your screen and over the top edges of your windows. Drag a window and it rides along; close it and it falls off. You can pick it up and throw it. Its eyes follow your mouse. When you're away, it sleeps.

**2. It watches along and joins in.**
It sees which app you use (never *what* you type) and adapts: a laptop for your code editor, headphones for music, popcorn for YouTube. Time counts too: coffee in the morning, a nightcap at night, a party hat at five on Friday, a beanie in winter.

**3. It helps, without nagging.**
The buddy mirrors your day. Work too long and *it* gets tired first and brings out a glass of water. Only if you ignore that does a speech bubble appear. Features live in its **backpack**: focus timer, reminders, music, quick notes, feeding it files, system status.

**4. It's entirely yours.**
- **Wardrobe:** buddy, colour, accessories.
- **Studio:** draw your own hats and glasses, or a whole buddy of your own, and share them as files. AI assistants can make them too.
- **Character:** calm, hyper, cheeky or sweet, and how often it talks.
- **Backpack:** which features are on.
- **Rules:** make your own "if … then …", like "at 12:30 it gets out its sandwiches".

It remembers things too: how long you've been buddies, and in its **diary** it writes one sentence every day.

## Ground rules

- **Small:** drawn at 32×32 pixels, shown 2 to 4 times as big. It talks mostly through animation and emotes, with text only when needed.
- **Never in the way:** clicks go through it except on the buddy itself; it hides for fullscreen apps and video calls.
- **Light:** next to no processor power while it sits still.
- **Private:** everything stays on your computer. No screenshots, and of your keyboard it counts the tempo at most.
- **No guilt:** it can't die and is never angry when you forget it.

## Art style

- Front view, stylised, no realistic animals. The Robot, the Ghost and the Sprout are the reference.
- Every buddy has its own silhouette; they mustn't look alike.
- One pixel of outline around every layer, light from the top left, three tones per colour (light, base, shade).
- Colours come from a palette, so every buddy comes in every colour.
- Accessories are layers with an anchor per species, so every hat fits every buddy. Where the general version doesn't fit a shape, a species gets its own version.
